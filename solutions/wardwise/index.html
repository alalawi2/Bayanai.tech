Nursing English practice before the conversation

Rehearse fictional ward conversations by listening, recording or typing, then keep useful phrases.

All solutionsWardwise

The purpose

Built around
a real question.

It can be easier to find the right words in a quiet rehearsal than in the middle of a shift. Wardwise offers short fictional situations for practising clear responses.

For: Nurses and healthcare professionals who want to practise workplace conversations in English.

  1. 01
    Guided practiceListen to a partner and rehearse a response aloud or in text.
  2. 02
    Optional AI partnerWith consent, send confirmed words and receive a contextual spoken reply.
  3. 03
    Phrase notebookSave useful language on your device and revisit it later.

How to engage

From interest
to use.

01 / 03

Choose a fictional case

Start with a handover, clarification or patient conversation.

02 / 03

Say or type your response

Record and replay, or use typed practice when speaking is not convenient.

03 / 03

Review and retry

Keep a phrase that helps and practise it again.

Wardwise is language practice, not medical advice or a clinical assessment. Never enter real patient information. Cloud features have daily free limits.
